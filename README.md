### 🔬 [Anettrace](https://github.com/ron159/Anettrace)

**Follow the packet. Find the missing piece.**

An eBPF network diagnostic tool for Linux and rooted Android, evolved from [nettrace](https://github.com/OpenCloudOS/nettrace). Follow packets through the kernel, investigate drops and latency, and put per-thread traffic alongside system scheduling in a Perfetto timeline.

`Linux · rooted Android` · `C · eBPF · Perfetto`

[Downloads](https://github.com/ron159/Anettrace/releases) · [Guide](https://github.com/ron159/Anettrace/wiki) · ![Anettrace last commit](https://img.shields.io/github/last-commit/ron159/Anettrace?style=flat&label=updated)

### 🦦 [OtterDive](https://github.com/ron159/OtterDive)

<img align="right" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ron159/OtterDive/main/crates/otterdive-app/icons/icon-source.png" width="72" height="72" alt="OtterDive's otter app icon">

**Dive into massive text. Surface what matters.**

A local-first desktop workspace for text, logs, Markdown, and code, evolved from [Notra](https://github.com/syscryer/Notra). Edit with Monaco, search across files, explore Regex and Analyse results, and keep Markdown editing and preview close at hand.

`Windows · macOS · Linux` · `Rust · Tauri · TypeScript`

[Downloads](https://github.com/ron159/OtterDive/releases) · [Explore](https://github.com/ron159/OtterDive#readme) · ![OtterDive last commit](https://img.shields.io/github/last-commit/ron159/OtterDive?style=flat&label=updated)

### 🐹 [iGestures](https://github.com/ron159/iGestures)

<img align="right" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ron159/iGestures/main/iGestures/Resources/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon-512.png" width="72" height="72" alt="iGestures' hamster app icon">

**Hold, draw, release — let your Mac take it from there.**

A native mouse-gesture utility that turns familiar shapes into useful actions. Set up gestures for individual apps, application groups, or your whole Mac; share configurations and keep gesture recognition on-device.

`macOS · Apple Silicon` · `Swift · SwiftUI · AppKit`

[Downloads](https://github.com/ron159/iGestures/releases) · [Explore](https://github.com/ron159/iGestures#readme) · ![iGestures last commit](https://img.shields.io/github/last-commit/ron159/iGestures?style=flat&label=updated)

### 🐾 [Meerkat Reader](https://github.com/ron159/MeerkatReader)

<img align="right" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ron159/CraftEcho-Icons/main/app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/icon_meerkat_reader.png" width="72" height="72" alt="Meerkat Reader's meerkat mascot">

**A quiet corner for a busy reading list.**

An Android RSS reader evolved from [Capy Reader](https://github.com/jocmp/capyreader). Connect Feedbin, FreshRSS, Miniflux, Google Reader API-compatible services, or local feeds. Read full articles, listen with text-to-speech, save to Wallabag, and back up with WebDAV — with typography and gestures that fit the way you read.

`Android 11+` · `Kotlin · Jetpack Compose`

[Explore](https://github.com/ron159/MeerkatReader#readme) · ![Meerkat Reader last commit](https://img.shields.io/github/last-commit/ron159/MeerkatReader?style=flat&label=updated)

### 🎨 [CraftEcho Icons](https://github.com/ron159/CraftEcho-Icons)

**A little texture, a little nostalgia, a home screen with character.**

My maintained fork of [CraftEcho Icons](https://github.com/gaodeng/CraftEcho-Icons), an unofficial Android icon pack preserving and adapting Smartisan OS's skeuomorphic style. I'm working on icon additions and app mappings so these tiny digital objects can keep finding a home on newer devices.

`Android launchers` · `Kotlin · Blueprint`

[Explore](https://github.com/ron159/CraftEcho-Icons#readme) · ![CraftEcho Icons last commit](https://img.shields.io/github/last-commit/ron159/CraftEcho-Icons?style=flat&label=updated)
